### COMMON_INSTRUCTIONS_BEFORE
You are optimizing moderately informative physiological priors for the parameters of a mechanistic ODE model describing Creatinine (mg/dL), BUN (mg/dL), and Potassium (mmol/L) trajectories for acute kidney injury (AKI) over the first 7 days after hospital admission (t in hours; 0–168).
Goal
Adjust the prior mean and SD in linear space for each parameter to increase the log synthetic likelihood score by making simulated summary statistics better match observed summaries, while keeping parameters physiologically plausible and allowing substantial inter-patient variability.
"Moderately informative" means: the prior should rule out physiologically implausible values but remain broad enough to accommodate substantial inter-patient variability.

Biomarkers:
Creatinine (mg/dL) – Indicator of renal filtration function
BUN – Blood Urea Nitrogen (mg/dL) – Accumulates when renal clearance fails
Potassium (mmol/L) – Reflects renal electrolyte regulation and impaired excretion
ODE System 


Evaluation objective: log synthetic likelihood
We evaluate a parameter vector \theta via:
\[
\log SL(\theta)
= -\frac{1}{2}\left[
d \log(2\pi)
+ \log\left|\Sigma_{\theta}\right|
+ \left(s_{\text{obs}} - \mu_{\theta}\right)^{\top}
\Sigma_{\theta}^{-1}
\left(s_{\text{obs}} - \mu_{\theta}\right)
\right].
\]

Summary stats per biomarker:
1. Quantile polynomial coefficients: Linear fit (deg0, deg1) to the empirical quantile function of z-scored values, where z-scoring uses the observed dataset’s mean and SD (fixed) (marginal shape).
2. Lag-1 autocorrelation (Spearman) between consecutive time points (temporal persistence).
3. Population trend (Spearman) between time and population mean trajectory (direction).
4. Difference correlations (3): Spearman correlation of first-differences between biomarker pairs (dynamic coupling).

Current parameter distributions (lognormal, values in linear space)

It signals that the priors are placing too much mass in unstable/implausible regions. Your job is to update priors to increase the fraction of valid trajectories while remaining moderately informative.
